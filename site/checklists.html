Checklists

Condensed, printable checklists for every chapter. Work top to bottom; each item points back to its chapter.

Decide: hardcover or not (ch. 1)

  • [ ] Answered in writing: who specifically pays $10–15 more for a hardcover, and why?
  • [ ] Checked the target buyer is real: libraries, gift buyers, event attendees, or confirmed reader requests.
  • [ ] Confirmed page count and trim fit KDP's hardcover limits (verify current list).
  • [ ] Estimated royalty per unit at a sane hardcover price with KDP's cost calculator.
  • [ ] Decided paperback-first is acceptable if the answer above is weak.

Choose the finish (ch. 2)

  • [ ] Decided case laminate vs. jacket using the five questions (desk use? gift? signing? margin? durability signal?).
  • [ ] Downloaded KDP's cover template for the chosen trim and finish.
  • [ ] Ran the jacket cost delta through the calculator (illustrative ~$1.50/unit at 300 pages).
  • [ ] Confirmed you can hit the minimum list price for the finish without embarrassing the book.
  • [ ] Drafted the case art (plain color is fine and traditional under a jacket).

Expanded distribution decision (ch. 3)

  • [ ] Understood ED ≠ "going wide"; decided this guide's ED is only about print reach.
  • [ ] Ticked the ED checkbox on the pricing page after files and price were settled.
  • [ ] Checked the ED-enforced price floor against your target price.
  • [ ] Re-derived the ED per-unit royalty from KDP's current rates and your list price.
  • [ ] Set a calendar reminder to sanity-check ED royalties in the next report cycle.

Preorder, or calmly skip it (ch. 4)

  • [ ] Files are genuinely final before enabling a preorder (no "fix during preorder").
  • [ ] Release date chosen for safety, not ambition.
  • [ ] One locked price per format; no planned increases.
  • [ ] Formats aligned: same date, or clearly labeled stagger.
  • [ ] Launch-week texts written before the preorder starts.
  • [ ] If nothing is final and there's no audience: skipped preorder without guilt.

Author copies (ch. 5)

  • [ ] Confirmed author-copy availability for the format and marketplace (hardcover may be US-only — verify).
  • [ ] Ordered one test copy before any bulk order.
  • [ ] Calculated per-unit cost including shipping.
  • [ ] Event plan: both formats, price sign, cash box, card reader, 10–20% extra stock.
  • [ ] Mailout plan: capped list, personal postcard, budget written down before stamping.
  • [ ] Giveaway plan: niche-community placement, budget line, one ask (a review line).
  • [ ] File the invoice as inventory expense per your bookkeeping process.

Royalty records (ch. 6)

  • [ ] Royalty ledger started (date, units, channel, take).
  • [ ] Tax info consistent with your entity naming on KDP.
  • [ ] Understood ~60-day payment lag and noted your first expected payout month.
  • [ ] Remembered the column headers: 70% is ebook-only; print is a rate minus printing cost; ED is a lower rate.
