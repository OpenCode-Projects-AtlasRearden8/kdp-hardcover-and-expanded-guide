# Glossary

Plain-English definitions for the terms this manual leans on.

**Author copies** — Physical copies of your own KDP book that you buy at printing cost, without royalty calculation. Your channel for signings, mailouts, and giveaways.

**Case laminate** — A hardcover without a dust jacket; the cover art is printed directly on the hard case. Durable, library-friendly, and cheaper per unit than jacketed hardcovers.

**Cover template** — KDP's downloadable sizing guide for a given trim and format. Spine width and bleed are computed from page count; always work from the current template.

**Delivery fee** — A per-megabyte fee deducted from ebook royalties in the 70% tier. Print books have no delivery fee; their tension is the *printing cost*.

**Expanded distribution (ED)** — A KDP option that makes paperbacks and hardcovers orderable through third-party wholesalers, extending reach to bookstores and libraries. Sold with a lower royalty rate than Amazon-marketplace sales. Not related to selling ebooks "wide."

**Going wide** — Selling your books directly on multiple retail platforms (Apple Books, Google Play, Barnes & Noble, Kobo, etc.), usually via a distributor or direct accounts. Different from expanded distribution. See *Publishing Beyond KDP Guide*.

**Hardcover (case with jacket)** — A hardcover whose hard case is wrapped in a printed dust jacket with flaps. Two cover-related files; higher per-unit cost; the "store hardback" look.

**Illustrative numbers** — Round, invented figures used in this guide's worked examples so the *method* is teachable. Not real prices; always re-derive from KDP's current calculator and rate pages.

**ISBN** — International Standard Book Number, a per-edition serial number. Each format (Kindle, paperback, case laminate, jacketed hardcover) needs its own identifier; KDP can assign a free one.

**List price** — The price you set on KDP; the base all royalty math starts from. Channels afterwards take their percentage and subtract their costs.

**Page count floor** — KDP minimums and maximums per format. For hardcovers the minimum is higher than for paperbacks (verify the current number).

**Price floor (minimum list price)** — The lowest list price KDP allows for a format, derived from printing cost plus minimum royalty. Rises with hardcover finishes and with expanded distribution constraints.

**Preorder** — A KDP option letting readers reserve a book before the release date and have it shipped then. Runs up to 90 days ahead (verify), and can be canceled or penalized by Amazon for mid-preorder price increases on regular books.

**Printing cost** — KDP's per-unit charge to print and bind one copy, based on page count, trim size, and format/finish. Deducted from every print sale's royalty, and also what you pay for author copies (plus shipping).

**Returnability / returns** — The wholesale channel's norm that bookstores can return unsold copies for credit. A returned copy reverses the sale (and the royalty) but, at beginner scale, net impact is usually small.

**Royalty** — The money that lands in your account after the channel's percentage and the costs come off the list price.

**Run-of-show** — A pre-written sequence of steps for an event (here: preorders and launches). The calm-launch habit: decide everything in advance, execute quietly, react to readers rather than to numbers.

**Trim size** — The physical page dimensions of a book (e.g., 6 × 9 inches). Hardcovers support fewer trims than paperbacks.
